### Title

Gap-filling and Flux-Balance Analysis of the Chlamydomonas reinhardtii Genome-Scale Metabolic Model iCre1355 for Improving Ergosterol Biosynthesis


### Abstracts

The synthesis of bioprospective compounds is limited to random strain mutations or under selective pressure for forward genetics objectives. As a result of supply chain instability, the active progesterone ingredient, ergosterol is presented as a new attractive candidate for overexpression in metabolically engineered algal systems. Predicted changes to chloroplastic methylerythritol phosphate (MEP) pathway, producing isopentenyl pyrophosphate (IPP) and dimethylallyl pyrophosphate (DMAPP) produce the sterol precursor and likely non-chloroplastic bottleneck: squalene. We hence present the groundwork for extending and modelling results of the ergosterol biosynthesis pathways to the validated genome-scale metabolic model (GSMM) iCre1355. This extension includes changes in the production of ergosterol and predictions of an upper-bound flux using enzyme-constrained flux-balance analysis. Increasing the production of ergosterols in C. reinhardtii may offer a sustainable supply of progesterone to address the ongoing global shortage. iCre1355 was first extended via literature-guided gap-filling to introduce the ergosterol/7-dehydroporiferasterol pathway and growth coupling from Chlorella sp. homologs. Validation includes growth predictions across trophic modes and flux analysis under nitrogen deprivation and light shifts, benchmarked against the original iCre1355 data. The sterol fluxes were then compared against the C. reinhardtii GC-MS profile for a sterol-targeted validation. The same model was used for flux-balance analysis to compare in-silico efficiency of the transgenic models. The results of the model identify Squalene epoxidase (SQE) as the primary rate-limiting enzyme. Independently increasing Squalene synthase (SQS) had a negligible impact. Notably, combining increased SQE production with a heterologous mevalonate (MVA) pathway increased theoretical ergosterol flux 7.9-fold, with Botryococcus braunni SQE enabling higher predicted fluxes. Further expansions to iCre1355 for other bioprospectives derisks projections of algal systems in research and venture capital. The greatest impact of this methodology can demonstrate the potential for efficient production of high-value products in transgenic microalgae and the accuracy of experimental characterization in C. reinhardtii.
